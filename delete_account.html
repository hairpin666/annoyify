Account

Delete Your Account

Last updated: August 6, 2026

You can permanently delete your Annoyify account and all associated data at any time, either from within the app or by emailing us. Deletion is irreversible.

Option 1: Delete In-App

  1. Open the Annoyify app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm deletion twice when prompted.

Your account and data are deleted immediately — no waiting period.

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

If you no longer have the app installed or can't sign in, email us from the address associated with your account and we'll delete it for you within 7 days.

Email your deletion request to:

info@annoyify.com

What Gets Deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your selected habits and annoyance level preferences
  • Notification history and delivery logs
  • Article view history and check-in / streak data
  • Device push notification tokens
